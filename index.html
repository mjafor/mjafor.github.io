Context: Urban Public Transit Systems in Dhaka

This empirical research investigates how traditional service marketing dimensions (Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence) directly and indirectly influence passengers' long-term ridership intentions, exploring customer satisfaction as a critical mediating path.
